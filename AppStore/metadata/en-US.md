## Description [4000]
Toolbox Studio is a small workshop for everyday media jobs. Drop a file onto the canvas, add the tools you need and connect them with wires. Each step shows a live preview, so you see the result while you change a slider. Press Render and the finished files land in a folder.

PHOTOS
• Resize, crop, rotate and flip
• Adjust exposure, contrast, saturation and warmth
• Dither with retro palettes: Game Boy, CGA, amber, sunset and more
• Turn any picture into ASCII art, in color or in braille dots
• Lift the subject out of its background, on device
• Work on one photo or a whole folder at once

VIDEO
• Trim a clip or grab a single frame
• Every photo effect also works on video, frame by frame
• Export to H.264, HEVC, ProRes 422 or an animated GIF

SOUND
• Pull the soundtrack out of a video
• Trim, fade, normalize, mix to mono
• Export to M4A, Apple Lossless, WAV or AIFF

PDF
• Merge documents, pick or rotate pages
• Add page numbers or a watermark
• Turn pictures into a PDF, or a PDF into pictures
• Password-protect and compress on export

TEXT
• Copy the text out of photos and scanned PDFs, recognized on device

MADE FOR THE MAC
• Eight ready-made workflows to start from
• Undo and redo, autosave, light and dark mode
• Under 10 MB, built on macOS's own Core Image, AVFoundation, Vision and PDFKit

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything runs on your Mac. No account, no sign-in, no tracking, no data collected.

## App Review notes
No account or sign-in is needed. To try it quickly: open the app, pick a workflow in the welcome window
(for example "Cut out a subject"), drop any photo onto the Image node and press Render (⌘R).
Files are saved to ~/Downloads/Toolbox Studio, or to a folder you choose in Settings.
