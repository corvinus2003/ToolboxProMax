# Privacy Policy

This policy covers **Toolbox Studio** (Mac App Store) and **Toolbox Pro Max** (GitHub), both made by Corvin Ghita.
Last updated: 20 November 2026.

## The short version

The app collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What happens to your files

- The photos, videos, sound and PDFs you add are processed only on your Mac, with tools built into macOS
  (Core Image, AVFoundation, Vision and PDFKit). They are never uploaded anywhere.
- Text recognition and "Lift Subject" run on device.
- The finished files are saved where you choose (by default the app's folder in Downloads).
- The app remembers your graph and which files you added, so it can reopen them next time. That information
  stays on your Mac, in the app's own storage, and you can delete it at any time by removing the nodes or
  deleting the app.

## Network

Toolbox Studio (the Mac App Store edition) does not connect to the internet.

Toolbox Pro Max (the GitHub edition) connects to the internet only when you use its Download node: it fetches
the link you paste, and it downloads the yt-dlp tool from GitHub the first time and refreshes it weekly. No
information about you is sent apart from what those requests need to work, such as your IP address, which the
sites you download from can see as with any web request.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does.
